For years, I thought my worth was tied to a white coat.

I thought if I walked away, I'd be nothing. I thought the title was the only thing that made me valuable. I thought leaving meant I was weak.

None of that was true.

## The Weight of Expectations

Medical school wasn't just hard academically. It was hard on my soul. It asked me to abandon parts of myself I didn't even know I was losing. It demanded everything and gave back very little.

And when I finally made the decision to leave, the silence was deafening.

Who was I without "future doctor" attached to my name?

## Rediscovering Myself

The answer came slowly.

I was someone who loved deeply. Someone who wrote to understand herself. Someone who found healing in soft mornings and quiet reflections. Someone who was *always* more than a career path.

> Leaving wasn't failure. It was the bravest thing I've ever done.

I chose myself. And I would choose myself again.

---

*Some versions of us survive things the current version no longer has to. And that is not weakness — that is grace.*
