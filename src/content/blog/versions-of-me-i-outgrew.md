There are versions of me that don't exist anymore.

The girl who apologized for everything. The one who made herself small to be loved. The version who thought suffering silently was a virtue. The me who believed my needs were a burden.

I miss parts of her.

Her hope. Her softness. Her ability to trust without hesitation.

But I don't miss the life she had to survive in.

## The Metamorphosis

Becoming is not a single event. It happens in layers. In quiet decisions. In therapy sessions. In journal entries written at 2 AM. In saying "no" when you used to say "yes" to everything.

It happens in choosing yourself — over and over and over again.

> I am not who I was. And I am not yet who I will be. I am in the beautiful, painful, sacred middle.

## Honoring Past Selves

I've learned to honor her — the old me — without wanting to be her again. She got me here. She survived so I could thrive.

And now it's my turn.
